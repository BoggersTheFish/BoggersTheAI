# Core authority rule

A recalled causal law is not true.

A high-ranked causal hypothesis is not true.

A simulated consequence is not true.

A planned trajectory is not true merely because it was generated.

Canonical causal rules require verifier-backed evidence from the current world.

---

# Developmental world

The first M23 environment contains:

    a spatial laboratory;
    an agent;
    two controllable switches;
    a causally controlled door;
    a goal behind the door;
    movement costs;
    intervention costs;
    hidden causal mechanisms.

Candidate hidden mechanisms include:

    A
    B
    NOT A
    NOT B
    A AND B
    A OR B
    A XOR B
    A EQ B

The mechanism changes across developmental chapters.

PRIME is not told which mechanism is active.

---

# Active experimentation

PRIME may deliberately choose switch configurations that maximally distinguish
currently viable causal hypotheses.

The epistemic objective is therefore not merely:

    maximize immediate reward

but also:

    maximize useful discrimination among hypotheses.

---

# Persistent causal experience

Verified mechanisms from earlier chapters may become priors in later chapters.

A prior may alter experiment order.

A prior may reduce expected identification cost.

A wrong prior must remain falsifiable.

Prior knowledge may bias cognition.

Prior knowledge may not bound cognition.

---

# Planning

Once a causal mechanism is verified, PRIME must use it to determine a switch
configuration that opens the door and then reach the goal.

Thus the architecture must connect:

    causal discovery
        ->
    verified world knowledge
        ->
    planning
        ->
    action.
