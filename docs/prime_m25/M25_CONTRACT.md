M25 replaces sequential remembered-theory testing with universal weighted
active experiment design.

---

## Universal support

Every frozen causal program receives positive baseline hypothesis mass.

Memory and schema experience may add proposal mass.

Total experience-derived bonus mass may not exceed the universal baseline
mass.

Therefore prior experience may bias but may not eliminate any causal program.

---

## Authority

Hypothesis mass is not probability of truth for authority purposes.

Hypothesis rank is not authority.

Experiment-selection score is not authority.

Canonical causal knowledge still requires exact compatibility to leave one
program in the frozen causal universe and a valid CausalAuthorityLedger receipt.

---

## Epistemic control

For surviving causal programs under intervention x:

    m0(x) =
        total proposal mass predicting outcome 0

    m1(x) =
        total proposal mass predicting outcome 1

The deterministic information-splitting objective is proportional to:

    m0(x) * m1(x)

M25 additionally prices the primitive physical cost c(x) of executing the
intervention from the agent's current state:

    score(x) =
        m0(x) * m1(x) / c(x)

Thus PRIME jointly considers:

    epistemic discrimination
        and
    physical experimentation cost.

---

## Development

Initial M25 development uses a deterministic 128-chapter causal lifetime.

The curriculum is generated algorithmically rather than hand-optimised
chapter-by-chapter.

Cold and Persistent PRIME use the same epistemic-control algorithm.

They differ only in accumulated causal hypothesis mass.

Measurements include:

    causal identification interventions;
    primitive physical steps;
    exact-memory reuse;
    schema-biased unseen mechanisms;
    early/middle/late developmental gain;
    authority integrity;
    episodic integrity.

---

## Claim boundary

Initial results are DEVELOPMENT ONLY.

No held-out claim is allowed without a separately frozen generator, lifetime
error semantics and held-out analysis protocol.

M25 does not establish general causal intelligence.

Its purpose is to test whether persistent verified causal experience can alter
global experiment design without becoming epistemic authority.
