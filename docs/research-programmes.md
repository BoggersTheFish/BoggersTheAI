# Thinking System Active Research Programmes

The `research/` directory houses bounded research programmes advancing the theoretical and experimental foundations of Thinking System:

---

## Active Research Programmes

1. **`research/structural-calculus/`**
   * Structural calculus for representation-dependent computation and quotient space mapping.

2. **`research/exodus/`**
   * Representation-dependent computation, semantic orbits, and intervention analysis in language models (ported from `ts-exodus`).

3. **`research/genesis/`**
   * Verifier-grounded semantic-orbit training and correctness flip reduction (ported from `ts-lm-genesis`).

4. **`research/observer-birth/`**
   * Formalization of observer families and residual localization dynamics.

5. **`research/cognitive-physics/`**
   * Wave dynamics, wave cycle plans, and field measurements.

6. **`research/substrate-experiments/`**
   * Hardware-aware quantization (`tsq`) and OpenCL GPU compute experiments (`TensionForge`).
